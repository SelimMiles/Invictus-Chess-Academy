The Invictus Method

Find the weakness. End it.

Every player has a recurring mistake that costs them the same game, over and over. My coaching is built to find it fast and rebuild it — in this order.

1.e4

Diagnose

We start with a real game between us, then break it down together. No guessing — we identify exactly where your rating is leaking points.

1...e5

Calculate

Targeted tactics and calculation drills on the specific pattern you're missing, not a generic puzzle set.

2.Nf3

Attack

Opening ideas and initiative built around how you actually play, so your preparation feels natural under pressure.

2...Nc6

Convert

Turning winning positions into wins — and the mental preparation to hold your nerve when a tournament is on the line.
